# 💫 About Me:
I'm a Computer Engineering student at Baku Higher Oil School (BHOS) who builds full-stack web apps, with a strong interest in backend systems. I work with React, Node.js, TypeScript and Supabase, and I like taking a project from database design all the way to a deployed product. <br><br>Outside of class I take part in hackathons, where I get to build fast, work in a team and solve real problems under pressure. In 2026 I placed 5th at the NASA Space Apps Challenge and 3rd at the GDG Baku & Holberton hackathon, and I completed an internship at Globtel Solutions, working with IoT fleet management systems.<br><br>My goal is to keep building real products and grow into a strong backend / AI engineer.
